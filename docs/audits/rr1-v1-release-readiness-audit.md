# RR-1 — v1 Release Readiness Audit (superseded)

**Status:** Superseded by **PFB-1 — Product Foundation Baseline Audit**.

The original RR-1 draft framed an Aspire/Manage.ps1-operable baseline as near “ready after small fixes” / narrow v1 release readiness. That framing was **too strong** for the product roadmap.

Canonical document:

→ [`docs/audits/pfb1-product-foundation-baseline-audit.md`](pfb1-product-foundation-baseline-audit.md)

Interpretation going forward:

- Stabilization S1–S13 and SWM-1–SWM-12 are a **product foundation baseline**.
- The platform is **foundation-ready for continued major product development**, not **release-ready**.
- Reserve **Release Readiness** for the final pre-ship phase after major product tracks complete.

Useful scorecard findings, checklists, and deferred surfaces from the RR-1 draft were **reframed and preserved** in PFB-1 — they were not discarded.
